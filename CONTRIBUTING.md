# Contributing to Diabetes Risk Prediction System

We welcome contributions to improve our healthcare AI system for diabetes risk prediction. Your expertise can help save lives through better early detection.

## 🏥 Medical Guidelines

### Healthcare Data Standards
- Follow HIPAA compliance guidelines for patient data protection
- Ensure all medical data is anonymized and de-identified according to healthcare standards
- Use only publicly available medical datasets or synthetic clinical data
- Include proper medical disclaimers and clinical limitations in all contributions

### Clinical Validation Requirements
- All model improvements must include comprehensive clinical validation metrics
- Provide sensitivity and specificity measurements for medical accuracy assessment
- Include false positive/negative rate analysis for clinical decision support
- Document any bias considerations in medical predictions and healthcare disparities
- Validate against established clinical benchmarks and medical literature
